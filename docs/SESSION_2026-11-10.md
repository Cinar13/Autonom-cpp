# Session 2026-11-10: optimising the MPPI race tuning "K"

Branch `optimised`. Follows `docs/SESSION_2026-11-09.md` (sections 3 and 6), where K was found.
Goal set during the session: race laps under 10 s (the calculated bound is 5.3 s), by raising the speed; only
the MPPI controller's own parameters are tuned (no planner or RPP changes).

**K** = `RaceFollowPath` in `src/autonom_bringup/config/nav2_mppi.yaml`: PathFollowCritic 11, CostCritic
`near_collision_cost` 200, `wz_std` 1.0, 1.0 s horizon (`time_steps` 20), `batch_size` 1000, with the TurtleBot3 wall
margin of `robot_sim/config/nav2_mppi.yaml` (local `robot_radius` 0.13). RPP drives the mapping lap, `loop_patrol`
switches to MPPI when fast mode starts.

## 1. Method

- `scripts/start_sim.sh robot:=tb3_fast --speed 1.2 --controller mppi` (NavFn), Gazebo GUI + RViz, one sim at a time.
- Measured over the 95 s of sim time after the `Fast mode` log line. Ground truth is the Gazebo model pose
  (`/world/default/dynamic_pose/info`, 60 Hz).
- **Laps**: forward crossings of the start line (x = 0 in the bottom corridor, 0.10 m hysteresis) in ground truth.
  The first lap time of every run starts at mapping speed, so **median / best** are taken over the flying laps
  (all but the first).
- **Failed**: `Lap N ended with ...` during the race. A failure in the first 5 s is counted separately: it is the
  RPP -> MPPI controller switch (`Failed to get result for follow_path in node halt`), the lap is re-sent at once
  and it happens with K too (3 of the runs below), so it says nothing about the variant.
- **Reverse**: reverse start-line crossings (ground truth; the patrol's own `REVERSE crossing` count agreed).
- **Mean speed**: ground-truth path length / 95 s. **Top**: 99th percentile of the speed over 0.2 s.
  **Stopped**: share of the time below 0.05 m/s.
- **Margin**: share of the time a wall is closer than 0.13 m. *GT*: robot centre against the wall footprints of
  `robot_sim/models/Fibonacci_arena/meshes/Part1.stl` (minimum distance in brackets). *LiDAR*: closest return of the
  simulated scan, the measure used on 2026-11-09 (the LiDAR sits 0.03 m behind the centre and sees a little less
  clearance, so it is the stricter of the two).
- **PF**: controller `Failed to make progress` aborts during the race.
- One parameter changed from K per run, K restored after each (rows marked "PathFollow 14 +" change one more
  parameter from K + PathFollow 14, see section 3). Single runs vary: the three K runs span 7-8 laps and a
  median lap of 12.2-13.6 s. A variant has to beat that spread, twice, before it counts.
- Adoption rule: more laps or more mean speed, without failed laps and without more than ~0.5 % of the time
  inside the 0.13 m margin.

## 3. What the runs say

<!-- findings -->
*Interim: the sweep is still running (wall penalty repeats and the `batch_size` fallback are open).*

- **No single MPPI parameter has beaten K so far.** Every variant that finished cleanly lies inside the spread of
  the three K runs (7-8 laps, median flying lap 12.2-13.6 s, mean 0.49-0.54 m/s).
- **The speed is there, the corners cannot take it.** PathFollow 14 / 17 and `vx_std` 0.4 reach 1.15-1.18 m/s on
  the bottom straight (K: 0.84-0.95) and single laps of 10.7-11.4 s, but then arrive too fast at the next corner:
  `Optimizer fail to compute path` (every sampled trajectory in collision), the robot stops (19-51 % of the time),
  progress failures, 0.8-3 % of the time inside the margin. The stops cluster just after the bottom-right corner
  (0.8, -0.2) and at the hairpin inside the U (-0.2..-0.4, 0.0).
- **Helping the corner entry did not rescue PathFollow 14**: more turn noise (`wz_std` 1.3) kept it moving but
  scraped the walls (min 0.097 m, 1.7 % inside the margin); a wider wall band (`near_collision_cost` 170) and a
  longer horizon stalled.
- **Planned braking must stay hard.** `ax_min` -1.5 (closer to the braking the robot actually showed, mostly
  ~1.3 m/s^2) froze the robot: the samples themselves need the 3 m/s^2 to stay out of the walls.
- **Optimiser settings** (`temperature`, `iteration_count`) change nothing measurable; `temperature` 0.1 stalls.
- `--speed 1.4` changes nothing because K never reaches the 1.2 m/s cap.

## 4. Other observations

- **Mapping lap stalled once on RPP** ("No reachable loop or frontier" after the middle hairpin, robot standing at
  about x -0.24, y -0.05 in the world). The variant under test only changed the race-time MPPI controller, so the
  mapping lap was plain RPP. `docs/SESSION_2026-11-09.md` says RPP never stalls there; this is one counter-example
  in about 30 mapping laps. The log of that attempt was overwritten by the relaunch; cause not investigated.
- **Wrong way round after a recovery** (`offset_from_furthest` 12): the robot stalled inside the U at about
  (0.2, 0.0), the planner aborted, BackUp and Spin ran, and Nav2 then drove the lap in reverse (4 reverse
  crossings). The rear gate did not stop it. Same pattern as section 2 of the 2026-11-09 notes; with K it did not
  occur in any run.
- **Controller-switch failure**: see "Failed" in section 1. Lap 2 is re-sent within 0.1 s, the robot does not stop.
- The Gazebo DiffDrive limits acceleration to 1.0 m/s^2 but not braking: ground truth showed up to +1.0 / -2.3 m/s^2.
  K itself accelerates at only 0.25-0.5 m/s^2 on the straights.
